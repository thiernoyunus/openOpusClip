# Approved trailers (worked examples)

Two trailers Yunus called "perfect" / "the absolute best". Study the *shape*, not the words: a new episode needs its own lines.
Their plan files live in the project's shared files (`doac-trailer-benchmark/scripts/bites_v3s.json`, `doac-podcast-trailer/test-ai-episode/plan-v4-colour.json` (v3 with the colour highlights)).

## 1. Guest episode: "Is AI actually making you money?" (81 s, guest 65%)

| Out | Role | Speaker | Line |
|-----|------|---------|------|
| 0 s | hook | **Guest** | "AI is not going to make you money." |
| 2 s | hook | **Guest** | "You're not going to open Claude and become a millionaire." |
| 4 s | back-and-forth | Host | "How would it help make money? Because I want to lose jobs. I want to pay less people." |
| 9 s | back-and-forth | **Guest** | "No, you're going to pay more people." |
| 12 s | sell-guest | Host | "Today I have brother Muhammad Azman, who is an AI veteran… in this space since 2018." |
| 19 s | frame | Host | "Is it actually providing solutions that are helping people make more money, or are we just burning tokens?" |
| 25 s | proof | **Guest** | "I asked AI to go through my past 18 months of online meeting content…" |
| 32 s | proof | **Guest** | "And then we built out the products and we sold it, and I'm still selling it." |
| 37 s | pushback | Host | "You know what's gonna make money? Go do your reps." |
| 40 s | value | **Guest** | The 10-80-10 rule: 10% human instructions, 80% AI, 10% human judging the work. |
| 58 s | value | **Guest** | "It can run your ads, it can monitor them and fix it on its own." |
| 63 s | pushback | Host | "I've heard these things over the last three years." |
| 67 s | stakes | **Guest** | "…what are you doing with AI? They're paying for ChatGPT. But they're wasting money." |
| 75 s | cliffhanger | Host | "Talk to me. Does it actually help me?" |
| 77 s | cliffhanger | **Guest** | "So firstly, I think people need to have a mindset shift…" → black |

**What makes it work:**
- **Cold open on the guest's most surprising claim.** The AI expert says AI won't make you money, and the line starts on "AI". The speaker's lead-in "What I mean by that is" was cut.
- **Guest and host trade lines before anyone is introduced.** The host pushes back and the guest doubles down. The introduction comes at 12 s, once you already want to know who this is.
- **The host's lines are short.** He asks, pushes back ("Go do your reps") and doubts ("I've heard these things"). The guest gets the long, valuable lines.
- **The trailer gives away something useful.** The 10-80-10 rule is a whole framework, and it runs 17 s, the one long bite.
- **The ending is the host's central question, and the guest starting to answer.** It cuts to black before the answer lands.
- **Every cut sits on a real pause.** The full "2018" plays, and no word is clipped at the end.
- **Almost every caption block has a coloured word, chosen by meaning.** Red "not" and a red box on "MONEY" in the hook, green "MILLIONAIRE" and "MORE", red "LESS" and "REPS", yellow "2018" and "10-80-10", a red box on "VETERAN" and "WASTING". The first render had only two gold words, and Yunus called the highlighting too rare.

## 2. Panel-style episode with a featured guest: "Is e-commerce an asset class?" (91 s)

The shape:
1. Hook: a bold claim ("give it 10, 20, 30 years… everyone and their mother is gonna be talking about e-commerce").
2. A challenge ("what gives you so much confidence to make such a bold statement?").
3. The premise ($10k a month within 90 days).
4. Sell the guest (three exits) and the skeptic (manages a quarter of a billion).
5. The question ("Is e-commerce an asset class?").
6. The guest's definition, and "absolutely yes".
7. Pushback ("not yet") and "merciless due diligence".
8. Risk: ad accounts shut down, "that's happened to me", the Facebook ban.
9. Cliffhanger: "Can my investment go to zero?", then black.

**What makes it work:**
- One topic from start to finish.
- The context comes early.
- Both sides are sold before they argue.
- The question the episode is built around is left unanswered.
